# Identification capture: allocation continuation rule

The first hourly allocations cannot contain the frozen 1,270 timed processes
after their separate diagnostics. The scheduler rejects increasing their
running time limit. This amendment precedes checkpointing or resuming them;
the initial pilots and a prefix of the timed schedule already ran. No timed
values have been used to choose cells, repetitions, parameters or thresholds.
The original complete condition manifest, shuffled schedule, five independent
process repetitions and all controls remain unchanged.

Checkpoint only at process boundaries. Stop the coordinating Python process,
let its current benchmark child exit, and verify an empty GPU process
inventory before terminating the coordinator. Preserve its entire raw tree.
A timed process without a successfully written record is repeated, even if
its CSV exists. The explicit checkpoint record and empty process inventory
replace the ordinary allocation-success marker for this intentional stop;
an unexpected termination or nonempty inventory voids that allocation.

Resume on the same physical node and GPU identities in a longer allocation.
Verify the same shared-library digest, compiled probe digest, toolkit,
driver, topology, environment overrides and fixed manifest. Repeat capability
pilots and selection observers in the new allocation. Reuse only successful
ordinary timing records with exact condition and schedule identity. Retain
each record's originating allocation, observer and environment evidence.
When selected geometry differs between allocations, label that contrast
unqualified and do not silently pool it as a controlled comparison.

Completeness is checked over the entire declared campaign, after joining the
two allocation inventories. Each allocation must independently satisfy its
identity, correctness, resource, process and termination guards. Report
allocation membership and observed clock ranges with every pooled summary;
also retain per-allocation summaries so a shift cannot hide in an average.
Missing repetitions remain incomplete, never additional passing points.

The continuation is identification evidence only. It does not alter the
fresh-validation bar, excuse an unexpected failure, or establish a fitted
hardware model. TRAF-54 and TRAF-43 remain open.
