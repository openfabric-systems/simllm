# A100 NVLink packet v1 expectations

## Freeze scope and chronology

This is the expectations-only record for TRAF-65. It is committed before the
hardware harness and before any timed TRAF-65 cell. No result from this study
may be written back into this file or `expectations.json`. A changed hypothesis
requires a new version and a new freeze.

The only hardware observations used here already existed before this freeze.
The
[A100 hardware envelope](../a100_hardware_envelope_v1/RESULTS.md) measured
94.00 to 94.07 GB/s for every four-link ordered pair and 281.65 GB/s for
three-way fan-out. This freeze treats those rows as the realized physical
envelope floors, while 100 GB/s per ordered pair, 25 GB/s per physical link,
and 300 GB/s per GPU direction are hard physical nameplate ceilings. The
candidate packet equation is

```text
100 * 256 / (256 + 16) = 94.1176470588 GB/s
300 * 256 / (256 + 16) = 282.3529411765 GB/s
```

Those two equations are declared candidate predictions. They are not TRAF-65
measurements.

## Model decision rules

**Packet overhead versus copy-engine coalescing.** The 256+16 explanation
survives only if raw-minus-data counter steps follow
`16 * ceil(payload / 256)` across persistent SM writes, dependent SM reads,
and copy-engine transfers, including alignment, lane-mask, stride, reuse, and
blind holdouts. If the 94 GB/s rate or the steps occur only for the copy engine,
or move with its message aggregation while the SM steps do not, the observation
identifies copy-engine coalescing and refutes packet-format authority.

**Bonding.** Four-link bonding requires four active links, no link above
25 GB/s, saturated balance within 10 percent, an ordered-pair copy-engine rate
inside the published 94.00 to 94.07 GB/s row, and three-way fan-out inside the
275 to 285 GB/s candidate band containing the published 281.65 GB/s row.
Stream-count-only scaling or idle links refutes packet striping across four
links.

**Credits.** A finite-credit result requires a reproducible first 95-percent
knee across outstanding-window, burst, gap, and recovery sweeps. The knee must
shift with packet wire bytes and recover after the frozen gap. A knee that
follows only producer occupancy is not credit evidence. Unit, window, and
return latency remain effective fitted quantities.

**FIFO placement and head-of-line blocking.** Other-peer interference from
one source identifies TX scope. Incast interference at one destination
identifies RX ingress or merge scope. Same-pair, direction, and region controls
localize blocking. A shared-cache-line-only effect is memory acceptance. The
A100 switch module is pass-through, so any extra byte, time, reordering, or
queue visit in that module is a model defect, not evidence for a switch FIFO.

## Fatal guards and acceptance

A fired guard voids the affected run. Guards are corruption, a non-NV4 or
fallback path, raw bytes below data bytes, a non-monotone counter, unexplained
nominal replay or error, a rate above any per-link, pair, or endpoint ceiling,
or contamination by throttling or another process.

Acceptance requires exact logical-byte and request/response conservation,
packet-overhead steps within one raw-counter quantum, held-out throughput and
completion error no larger than 10 percent or 1 microsecond, whichever is
larger, and the first saturation knee within one frozen sweep point. NCCL send
and receive are protocol-conservation validation only. They never define the
packet format.

The hardware run publishes a versioned candidate profile and conformance
fixtures. TRAF-65 remains OPEN until the later live held-out result satisfies
the registered closure chain. The analytic path and every uncalibrated
architecture remain exact selectable bypasses.
