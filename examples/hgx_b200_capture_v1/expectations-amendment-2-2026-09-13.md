# HGX B200 capture amendment 2, 2026-09-13

This expectations amendment is post-specified to an independent review
finding on the implemented slice and is frozen before the corrected join
and its rerun. The original freeze and the first amendment stay unchanged;
this file withdraws one control and adds a rule.

## Withdrawn control

Cell G5's last control stated that generation `"h100"` against the
eighteen-lane B200 dump succeeds because the lane counts match. That
control made the join lane-count-only: a B200 board could be bound to the
H100 preset, and an H200 board to the B200 preset, on lane count alone. The
review found that gap, and it contradicts the fail-closed rule every reader
and join in this module follows. The control is withdrawn, and the tracked
B200 results are regenerated by the corrected implementation.

## Added rule

`captured_switched_node` checks the generation's silicon before binding:
every GPU of the dump must report one of the generation's PCI device ids
and its streaming-multiprocessor version (`a100`: device `0x20b0` or
`0x20b2`, `sm` 80; `h100`: device `0x2330`, `0x2335` or `0x2339`, `sm` 90;
`b200`: device `0x2901`, `sm` 100), and a board whose GPUs do not all match
is refused. The check runs after the lane and switch-port checks, so every
existing refusal message stays byte identical. G5's last control becomes:
generation `"h100"` against the B200 dump is refused on silicon, and
`"a100"` stays refused on lanes. The join also refuses a switched-dump GPU
that is not under a PCIe switch and a PCIe switch nested in a PCIe switch.

## What does not change

Every other literal of G1 through G6, the three compatibility digests, the
bundle, the payload rate, the module-id slot rule and the evidence
accounting. The B200 study's `--check` reproduces the regenerated results,
whose implementation stamp names the corrected commit.
