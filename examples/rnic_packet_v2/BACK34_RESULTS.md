## Registered acceptance clause

BACK-34 requires: "add a registered partial-final-packet cell at the
production 4,096-byte wire quantum to the ABI-v2 packet study and the directed
composed runtime." The Tier A producer now registers an ABI-v2-only 5,000-byte
cell with a 64-byte header and 4,096-byte maximum wire packet. The directed
`SimllmAtlahsFlowRuntimeTest.AbiV2ProjectsRealPacketIssuesIntoTheNativeWqeTimeline`
uses the same geometry through the composed packetized physical runtime.

It further requires: "Use a nonmultiple payload, observe exact final payload
and wire bytes plus committed TX and RX boundaries." Both paths observe a
4,032-byte first payload in 4,096 wire bytes and a 968-byte tail in 1,032 wire
bytes. Tier A commits TX starts at 1,000 and 82,920 ps, TX finishes and RX
arrivals at 82,920 and 103,560 ps, and the terminal at 103,560 ps. The
composed physical runtime commits TX starts at 1,000 and 144,200 ps, TX
finishes and RX arrivals at 82,920 and 164,840 ps, deliveries at 164,840 and
185,480 ps, and the WQE terminal at 185,480 ps. The directed test requires
exactly one event of each lifecycle kind for each packet.

Finally, it requires: "require the full-quantum rows and the ABI-v1 artifacts
to remain exact." The old ABI-v2 observation, projected through the repository
`BypassArtifacts` record after removing only the newly registered tail cell,
matches every input and all four behavioral artifact classes. The accepted
ABI-v1 raw and summary artifacts retain their exact bytes.

All registered clauses are demonstrated. No residual BACK-46, BACK-47 or
BACK-48 entry is created.

## Scored compatibility and entailment

The genuine-risk compatibility result is 3 of 3:

- Full-quantum ABI-v2 projection: 1 of 1, with no changed input or behavioral
  artifact.
- ABI-v1 raw observation identity: 1 of 1.
- ABI-v1 summary identity: 1 of 1.

The compatibility comparisons run on generated bytes before the exact new
tail oracle. A producer can change the existing full-quantum projection after
it runs without violating any new tail field. Either ABI-v1 artifact can pass
the semantic Tier A gate and still differ byte for byte. All three relations
can therefore fail in a run that reaches them and are not entailed by an
earlier fatal check.

The exact 5,000-byte packetization, eight lifecycle rows, 968-byte payload
tail, 1,032-byte wire tail and all frozen times are component oracles and are
fatal unscored. The three reference digests checked at entry are
by-construction change-set guards and are also unscored.

`37a4e9cf88a1b60094409150dfad25599eb77cbf268b3d08bfacf527e493a26a`
and `00ef7e4f5bdbd38f4eabe9ba42dc75f56de528c8751b93e6eef4a3089fa61004`.
