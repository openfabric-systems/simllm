## Frozen cell

The new ABI-v2-only cell uses a 5,000-byte WQE, 400 Gbit/s link, 1,000 ps
doorbell service, 4,096-byte maximum wire packet, 64-byte data header, and
zero propagation. The payload quantum is therefore 4,032 bytes.

Packet 0 has payload offset 0, payload 4,032, and wire size 4,096. It starts TX
at 1,000 ps and finishes at 82,920 ps. Packet 1 has payload offset 4,032,
payload 968, and wire size 1,032. It starts TX at 82,920 ps and finishes at
103,560 ps. Payload and wire sizes must be observed on TX start, TX finish, RX
arrival, and Delivered rows.

For the unbound Tier A port, RX and Delivered commit at the source finish, so
RX boundaries are 82,920 and 103,560 ps. The native WQE boundaries are first
TX 1,000, last TX 82,920, first RX 82,920, last RX 103,560, and outcome
103,560 ps.

For the directed packetized composition, the real destination serializer
commits RX at 164,840 and 185,480 ps. Its native WQE boundaries are first TX
1,000, last TX 82,920, first RX 164,840, last RX 185,480, and outcome
185,480 ps.

## Entailment and guard classification

The three compatibility comparisons can fail after the producer runs and are
evaluated from generated bytes before the new exact tail oracle. Their
genuine-risk fraction is reported separately. The three reference digest
checks made at entry are by-construction change-set guards and are unscored.
The exact new tail is fatal unscored because no independent behavioral claim
would remain after its fields are pinned.
